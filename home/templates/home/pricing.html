COMMON QUESTIONS

Pricing FAQs

Why don't you have fixed prices?

Tasks vary significantly in size and complexity, so a fixed price would not fairly reflect the amount of work required.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. You receive a quote after your task has been reviewed, allowing you to know the expected cost before the work proceeds.

What makes one task cost more than another?

Factors such as workload, complexity, deadline, specialised skills and the scope of the requested deliverables all influence the quote.

Can I submit a task just to get a quote?

Yes. The purpose of the review is to understand your requirements and provide a price that matches the work involved.
